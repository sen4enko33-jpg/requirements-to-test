Requirement Specification: The system will provide a way to merge goals into policies to reduce time spent on the goal reconciliation process. Origin: Customer interview (Qingfeng He) 02/06/04 Priority: 3 FR-GSM 11 Requirement Definition: The system shall provide the ability to display the number of occurrences of a goal in a policy, in multiple policies and within a domain.
