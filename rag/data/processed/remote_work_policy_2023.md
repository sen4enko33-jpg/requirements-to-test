# Remote Work Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains who may work remotely, how remote days are approved and which security rules apply to company laptops outside the office. It applies to all permanent employees of Northwind Logistics.

## 2. Eligibility

Employees who have completed the probation period may work remotely up to three days per week. Warehouse and dispatch roles are not eligible, because their work requires physical presence on site. Team leads may approve exceptions for a limited period.

<!-- page 2 -->

## 3. Approval

Remote days are requested in the HR portal at least two working days in advance. The team lead approves or declines the request within two working days. Recurring remote days are approved once per quarter.

## 4. Equipment

The company provides a laptop and a headset. Employees may claim up to 25 EUR per year for a keyboard or a mouse through the expense policy. Personal computers may be used for company work after approval by the IT service desk.

## 5. Information security

Company laptops must use full disk encryption. The corporate VPN is required when connected to any network outside the office; public Wi-Fi may be used only through the VPN. Lost or stolen devices are reported to the IT service desk within 24 hours.

<!-- page 3 -->

## 6. Working hours

Core hours are from 11:00 to 15:00 Kyiv time. Employees must be reachable in the team chat during core hours and attend scheduled meetings with the camera on when requested by the organiser.

## 7. Review

The policy is reviewed once every two years by HR. Questions about this policy go to hr@northwind.example.
