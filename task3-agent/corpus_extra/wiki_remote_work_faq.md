## Who answers questions about remote work?
Questions about remote work go to the HR service desk. The Remote Work Policy is the authoritative document; this wiki
page only collects frequent questions of employees and links to the policy.

## How do I book my remote days?
Remote days are booked in the HR portal at least one day in advance and approved by the team lead. Booked days can be
cancelled in the portal until 9:00 on the same day.
